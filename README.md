I'm an undergraduate student in the Ingeniería de Ciencia de Datos e Inteligencia Artificial program at SENATI in Perú. I am deeply passionate about turning raw data into actionable insights through **Machine Learning, Data Mining, and Advanced Data Analytics**. I have practical experience building data-driven solutions, working with spatial datasets, and integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) into workflows.

* 🚀 **Technical Experience & Highlights:**
  * **Geospatial Data Analytics:** Processed spatial coordinates (latitude, longitude) and `.geojson` data to dynamically map and analyze administrative regions, districts, and departments across Perú.
  * **LLMs & Agentic AI:** Implemented **Function Calling** pipelines using **Qwen** open-source models to seamlessly connect generative AI with external structured databases and APIs.
  * **End-to-End ML Pipelines:** Designed robust predictive architecture from synthetic data generation and outlier handling to hyperparameter optimization (Random Forest) and automated executive reporting.

- 🌱 I’m currently learning ...
